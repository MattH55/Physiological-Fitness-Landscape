  AGING & SENESCENCE (4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Alpha-Klotho
    Slug: alpha-klotho
    Units: pg/mL  |  Specimen: serum  |  Direction: higher_better
    Valid range: 0.0-2000.0 (optimal: 500.0)
    Anti-aging hormone. Strong inverse predictor of all-cause mortality. Low levels associated with
    accelerated aging, CKD, and cardiovascular disease. Genuinely undercovered in most panels.

  DNA Methylation Age Acceleration (PhenoAge/GrimAge)
    Slug: dna-methylation-age
    Units: years  |  Specimen: blood  |  Direction: lower_better
    Valid range: -20.0-20.0 (optimal: 0.0)
    Epigenetic clock. Among the best all-cause mortality predictors known. Age acceleration (difference
    between biological and chronological age) strongly predicts mortality independent of traditional
    risk factors.

  Leukocyte Telomere Length
    Slug: telomere-length
    Units: kb  |  Specimen: blood  |  Direction: higher_better
    Valid range: 3.0-10.0 (optimal: 6.0)
    Cellular aging marker. Short telomeres associated with increased all-cause mortality,
    cardiovascular disease, and cancer. Reflects cumulative cell division and oxidative stress.

  Mitochondrial DNA Copy Number
    Slug: mt-dna-copy
    Units: copies/cell  |  Specimen: blood  |  Direction: u_shaped
    Valid range: 0.0-10000.0 (optimal: 3000.0)
    Marker of mitochondrial function and cellular aging. Both very low and very high copy numbers
    associated with increased mortality. Reflects mitochondrial biogenesis and turnover.

  ANTHROPOMETRIC (4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Appendicular Lean Mass / Sarcopenia Index
    Slug: appendicular-lean-mass
    Units: kg/m2  |  Specimen: imaging  |  Direction: higher_better
    Valid range: 0.0-40.0 (optimal: 20.0)
    Skeletal muscle mass index. Low appendicular lean mass (sarcopenia) associated with increased

  Body Mass Index (BMI)
    Slug: bmi
    Units: kg/m2  |  Specimen: anthropometric  |  Direction: u_shaped
    Valid range: 10.0-80.0 (optimal: 22.0)
    One of the most-replicated U-shaped mortality curves. Both underweight (<18.5) and obesity (>30)
    associated with increased mortality. Optimal range ~20-25 in most cohorts.

  Gait Speed
    Slug: gait-speed
    Units: m/s  |  Specimen: functional  |  Direction: higher_better
    Valid range: 0.0-3.0 (optimal: 1.2)
    As strong or stronger than grip strength in elderly cohorts. Slow gait speed (<0.8 m/s) associated
    with significantly increased all-cause mortality. Marker of overall physiological reserve.

  Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR)
    Slug: waist-height-ratio
    Units: ratio  |  Specimen: anthropometric  |  Direction: lower_better
    Valid range: 0.0-2.0 (optimal: 0.5)
    Outperforms BMI in several meta-analyses for predicting cardiovascular and all-cause mortality.
    Cutoff >0.5 associated with increased risk regardless of height.
